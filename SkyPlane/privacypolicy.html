SkyPlane – Privacy Policy

Introduction

"Beer Manager" is an application designed to help users keep track of the number of drinks ordered and the number of drinks remaining at restaurants.
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Data Collection

Beer Manager does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information.
Information entered into the application, such as the number of drinks ordered, is stored and processed locally on the user's device.

Location Information

Beer Manager may use the device's location information to help users find nearby restaurants.
Location information is used only when necessary to provide this feature and is not stored by Beer Manager for tracking or advertising purposes.

Data Usage

Information entered by the user is used only to provide the application's features, such as tracking ordered and remaining drinks and displaying related information.

Third-Party Services

Beer Manager does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to track users.
When features that require access to external services are used, such as searching for nearby restaurants, network communication may be required.

Data Sharing

Beer Manager does not sell or share users' personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.
Any changes will be published on this page.
